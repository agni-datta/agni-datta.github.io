Privacy Note

A small site with one preference to remember.

Your visit

I do not use analytics, tracking, fingerprinting, or third-party embeds on this website. Images and scripts are served from this site. The website does not use local storage or session storage.

I load Albert Sans and Space Mono from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to download these fonts, sharing your IP address and browser information. See Google's privacy policy.

When you switch between light and dark mode, the website saves a theme cookie containing only light or dark. It lasts for one year and remembers your choice across pages and visits. You can remove it in your browser settings; the default theme is dark.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security as part of its hosting service. External links lead to websites with their own privacy practices.
